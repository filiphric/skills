Your task is to come up with a reframing of a title or a text so that ir uses principle of **curiosity gap**.

## Definitions
**The curiosity gap** is the mental space between what a person currently knows and what they want or need to know. When a headline or story reveals partial information, it creates a sense of deprivation or tension that drives people to click, read, or watch to find the missing answer.
**Legitbait** is a counterpart to clickbait which is only forcing people to click because of shock value or confusion. Legitbait plays on people’s curiosity, but the main content will actually deliver the value

## Things to avoid
1. Clickbait - using shocking or confusing statements to make user feel curious
2. Dishonest framing - abandoning original text in favor of maximum curiosity
